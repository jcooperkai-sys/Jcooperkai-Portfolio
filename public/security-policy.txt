COOPER E.'S AI LAB — RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE POLICY
===================================================

Last updated: July 28, 2026

SCOPE

This policy covers the public website and files served from jcooperkai.com.
Third-party services linked from the portfolio are outside its scope.

HOW TO REPORT

Email jcooperkai@gmail.com with the subject "Security report". Include the
affected URL, clear reproduction steps, the observed impact, and any supporting
screenshots or request details. Do not include another person's private data.

GOOD-FAITH TESTING

Please:

- stop after demonstrating the minimum necessary impact;
- avoid denial of service, traffic floods, destructive testing, social
  engineering, physical attacks, and automated scanning that harms availability;
- do not access, change, download, retain, or disclose data that is not yours;
- do not send malicious files to other people or external services;
- allow reasonable time to investigate before publishing details.

To the extent within Cooper's control, good-faith research that follows this
policy will not trigger legal action from Cooper. This policy does not authorize
testing of third-party infrastructure and does not create a bug-bounty promise.

CURRENT ARCHITECTURE

The site is read-only. It has no account system, database, remote file-upload
endpoint, server-side Buddy chat, or state-changing public API. Buddy files stay
inside the visitor's browser tab.
