COOPER E. — RESEARCH STATEMENT
==============================

OVERVIEW

I research agentic AI systems: programs that plan, use tools, remember
context, and take multi-step action toward a goal. My central question is how
to make these systems capable without making their behavior mysterious or
difficult to stop.

RESEARCH APPROACH

I prefer small, inspectable experiments. A useful prototype should expose its
state, record why it used a tool, and make failures easy to reproduce. I treat
interface design, permission boundaries, and recovery behavior as core
research problems rather than polish added after a model works.

CURRENT DIRECTION

My current work examines three connected areas: reliable agent workflows,
selective memory and retrieval, and human-centered controls for autonomous
actions. The projects on jcooperkai.com are independent working notes and
prototypes that document both the ideas and the unfinished edges.

FUTURE AGENDA

I want to build agents that can pursue longer tasks while remaining legible
to the people who use them. That includes better checkpoints, reversible
actions, useful uncertainty signals, and compact records that make an
interrupted task easy to understand and resume.

RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT

Powerful automation should not quietly expand its own authority. My approach
emphasizes bounded access, explicit state changes, privacy-conscious data
handling, and human confirmation when an action has meaningful external
consequences.
