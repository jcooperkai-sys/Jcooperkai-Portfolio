AI EXPERIMENT ARCHIVE
=====================

Status: ARCHIVE

AI Experiment Archive is a growing record of prompts, prototypes, failures,
and lessons from small AI systems built to answer one focused question at a
time. Older experiments remain visible so later work can be traced back to
the assumptions that shaped it.
