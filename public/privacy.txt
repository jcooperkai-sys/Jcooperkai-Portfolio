VISIT COUNTING
--------------
The visitor number on this page reflects unique visits per day. Your network address is never
stored. It is combined with a salt that changes every day and hashed with SHA-256, and only that
irreversible digest is kept, expiring automatically after 24 hours. Because the salt rotates, a
digest from one day cannot be matched against another. No cookie or browser storage is used, and
nothing about a visit is shared with any third party. Where no counting store is configured the
number shown is decorative.
