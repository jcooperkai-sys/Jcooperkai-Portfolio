PRIVACY AND LOCAL FILES, jcooperkai.com
=======================================

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This site has no accounts, forms, chat, comments, analytics, advertising
trackers, cookies or visitor counter. It does not use localStorage,
sessionStorage or any other browser storage.

WHAT THE PAGE LOADS

Every file the page loads (fonts, icons, badges, images, scripts) is served
from jcooperkai.com itself. The page makes no requests to any third party.

LOCAL FILES

Nothing is uploaded to this site and it accepts no files. Images in the
gallery are opened from this site's own copies.

HOSTING

The site runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes ordinary
request data (IP address, browser details, requested path and time) to deliver
the page and block abuse. A short-lived rate limit uses your IP address in
memory to decide whether a request is allowed. I keep no visitor log of my own
and sell nothing.

LINKS AND EMAIL

Links to GitHub, X and Webbify follow those sites' own privacy policies. The
email link opens your mail app, and the message is then handled by your email
provider and mine.
